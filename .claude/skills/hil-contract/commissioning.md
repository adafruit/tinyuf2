# Commissioning a TinyUF2 rig board

Part of the HIL contract (`SKILL.md` beside this file).

Commissioning is the lead's, never `hil-operator`'s or a review's. It runs under a manual hold, except for the re-entry run, which locks itself. Record each result in the board's commissioning note (agentrc `target-debug` project notes), and `uid`, `lock` and `reentry` in the roster. In this order:
- **Cabling:** cable the USB port TinyUF2 enumerates on (`BOARD_TUD_RHPORT`). It can differ from the one tinyusb examples use: USB OTG1 on imxrt1060_evk; USB USER (CN3) on stm32f303disco; the USB OTG FS Micro-AB connector, not the ST-LINK one, on stm32f411ve_discovery (UM1842). A DFU device "not seen" with no new device in `dmesg` is a cabling problem first. Keep the probe on a hub apart from the target's (SKILL.md Limits).
- **Silicon revision,** before any other result: read it from the chip marking or registers, and check that revision's errata through read-doc. RT1060's DIGPROG does not tell A from B, so the marking decides.
- **Port power** (a board with `usbPower`): before setting `usbPower`, check that `uhubctl` reports the hub's power switching as `ppps`, not `ganged`; a ganged hub keeps a gang's power on while another of its ports is powered and can switch the whole gang off with its last port (USB 2.0 11.11). `ppps` is a preliminary check only, since a hub with several gangs or no switches may report it too (USB 2.0 11.11, 11.11.1; the USB251xB's switching depends on its board, DS00001692 p22). The hub's port `disable` files writable by the runner's user, through `/etc/udev/rules.d/52-tinyuf2-hil-uhubctl.rules`, one line per hub: on `ACTION=="add|bind"` of its `DRIVER=="hub"` interface, matched by the hub's VID, PID and serial, `RUN` `chgrp plugdev` and `chmod g+w` on `/sys$devpath/*-port*/disable`. The runner refuses the board while that file is not writable. Check once that writing `disable` 1 switches the port off (`uhubctl -l <location> -p <port>` reads `off`), then write 0. Under a hold of every board on the hub, show before `usbPower` is relied on that VBUS on that port actually drops during the cycle, by evidence independent of the hub's own report (a meter on the port, or a power indicator on its switched VBUS going out, such as a bus-powered device's or one the hub's confirmed schematic places there), and that the cycle reaches no other port: the board on that port disconnects and re-enumerates with a new device number, and every other device on the hub keeps its device number (as measured on 5-3, bench-history.md: Shared hubs). `uhubctl` reading `off`, a disconnect and a new device number show only the port's logical state and a re-enumeration, which a hub with no power switch also gives (USB 2.0 11.11; the kernel's `disable` disconnects the device before it clears PORT_POWER).
- **Automount exclusion:** `/etc/udev/rules.d/99-tinyuf2-hil.rules`, one line per board, with the VID and PID from the board's source, in exactly this form (the runner accepts only this form, with exact `==` matches):

  ```
  SUBSYSTEM=="block", ATTRS{idVendor}=="<vid>", ATTRS{idProduct}=="<pid>", ATTRS{serial}=="<uid>", ENV{UDISKS_IGNORE}="1"
  ```

  Write it with the roster's `uid`, before the serial can be confirmed: `install` and `run` refuse without the rule, and a fresh board runs whatever tinyusb last flashed, not TinyUF2. A serial a running tinyusb example reports (`lsusb -v`) is an early cross-check, not a confirmation. Until the serial is confirmed, also inhibit automount for every board of this VID:PID with a temporary `/etc/udev/rules.d/98-tinyuf2-hil-commissioning.rules`:

  ```
  SUBSYSTEM=="block", ATTRS{idVendor}=="<vid>", ATTRS{idProduct}=="<pid>", ENV{UDISKS_IGNORE}="1"
  ```

  Then `sudo udevadm control --reload`.
- **Bootloader:** `install` a clean HIL record of HEAD under the hold and observe DFU (add `--try-reentry retained-register` if a valid app could keep DFU away).
- **USB serial:** confirm the enumerated serial equals `uid` (sysfs or `lsusb -v`), and record the USB port paths. If it differs, fix `uid` and the rule together, reload, and `install` again; its reset re-enumerates the board and udev applies the reloaded rules, so no replug should be needed (by mechanism, not yet observed on a uid fix).
- **Cleanup:** `restore` under the hold and observe DFU leave after the blinky copy. Record the reset notes J-Link prints on install (`BENIGN`).
- **Exclusion proof:** delete the temporary rule and reload before the re-entry run below; its `dfu` step passes only when `udevadm info` on the enumerated block device shows `UDISKS_IGNORE=1` (`check_exclusive`). A host rule that ignores a whole VID, such as tinyusb's `88-tinyusb.rules` for 239a, sets the same property, so the proof cannot tell it from the serial rule; the runner still requires the serial line.
- **Loader footprint:** for the flash target's `loadfile`, place sentinels just past the written range and elsewhere in its erase block. Confirm them from a fresh session before the operation under test, and again from another fresh session after it: a verify in the session that wrote them cannot show what a later operation destroys. Make the written data differ from what is on the flash, since a download whose data already matches is skipped ("Contents already match") and erases nothing. Record the result in bench-history.md.
- **Fixture prerequisites,** before anything executes the battery's fixture:
  - the app start meets VTOR's alignment for the fixture's vector table (`fixture()`);
  - its stack top, the `m_data` origin the runner reads (`ram_origin`) + 0x1000, is in RAM enabled when the bootloader hands off: the ROM default per read-doc, which TinyUF2 does not reconfigure (DTCM on imxrt1060_evk);
  - under a hold, a word just below that stack top takes a written value and reads it back, and then its saved value (0x20000FFC on imxrt1060_evk).
- **Re-entry:** commit the roster entry without `reentry`, build the record, release the manual hold, and run the battery in commissioning mode; it locks itself and ends with the cleanup, so it also proves the restore:

  ```bash
  python3 .claude/skills/hil-contract/scripts/hil_test.py run -b <board> --build-record cmake-build/hil/<board>.build.json --try-reentry retained-register test/hil/local.json
  ```

  `--try-reentry` is the lead's only, and is refused once `reentry` is set. The row's `reentryDecision` names the method when the run passed or failed only at `reenter`'s `CURRENT.UF2` readback, every observation of the tried method is true (`controlBootedApp`, `dfuCameUp` and `magicConsumed` for `retained-register`), and the board was cleaned up and not held. Set `reentry` to it and commit. A null decision leaves `reentry` unset: file a `followup` issue with the observations for diagnosis, not as a diagnosed bug. When the drive copy itself fails, a fixture installed by hand with J-Link `loadbin` can isolate re-entry from the copy for diagnosis; it never sets `reentry`.
- **On an esptool board,** in place of the J-Link items: cable the native USB connector as well as the bridge's; check once by hand that the chip has no secure boot, secure download or flash encryption and that its flash holds the candidate's layout (`esptool get-security-info`, `esptool flash-id`), since the runner never checks them; record the bridge serial and the chip's MAC (`uid`, TinyUF2's USB serial); `install` from the record and observe DFU, then `restore` and observe DFU leave. Re-entry is `--try-reentry dfu-detach`, whose decision needs `detachAcked`, `appLeft` and `dfuCameUp`; before setting it, repeat DFU_DETACH ten times under the hold and check the host log shows no USB reset on the DETACH transitions (USB DFU 1.1 bitWillDetach).
- **An `openocd_esp` route** (Hosts/rosters) stays `"commissioning": "pending"` until the lead has checked, with read-doc, that the chip has a USJ, how it is routed against OTG and what its resets do, and has measured, with the battery's exact firmware and operations, that the USJ stays available, then run the route's `install` and `restore` under a hold. Clear the key and commit.
- **J-Link transcript shape:** that `regs` and `mem32` output parses (`after()`, `regs()`), and which reset notes are benign. A benign note is exempted by device and exact text in `BENIGN`: on MIMXRT1062xxx6A, "Could not find valid reset vector." followed by "Target halted on flash header read.", printed on every reset while the app is invalid; on MIMXRT1011DAE5A the same note on every reset of a TinyUF2 image; on stm32f303vc, "Active write protection detected.", "Device will be unsecured now.", "Failed to unlock device." and "Can not attach to CPU. Trying connect under reset.", the connect that removes TinyUF2's write protection (SKILL.md Limits). On stm32f411ve the same connect prints "Active write protection detected.", "Device will be unsecured now." and "Device unlocked successfully.", which match no failure pattern and need no exemption; only the flash target connects as that device (SKILL.md Limits). A reset session (`w4` to AIRCR) passes only once J-Link reports the write issued ("Writing VALUE -> ADDR") with no failure before it. In a hand-written session, `-ExitOnError 0` still exits at a failed verify, before any later command runs, and leaves the target halted: resume it from a new session (`g`, or `r` then `g`), or keep verifies in a session ahead of the one that resumes.
